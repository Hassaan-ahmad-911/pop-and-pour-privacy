Pop & Pour – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Pop & Pour ("the game", "we", "us") is a color-sorting puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information is collected when you play, why, and the choices you have.

In short

  • There are no user accounts. You never sign in or give us your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not collect personal data directly. Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game uses services from Google (AdMob, Firebase and Google Play) that collect some information, as described below.
  • The game is intended for users aged 13 and older.

Information saved on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, themes, decorations, daily challenge streak), your settings and the player name and avatar you choose are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: banners, ads between levels, and rewarded ads you choose to watch. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are shown, and personalized ads are only shown if you consent. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choices.

In other regions, Google may show personalized ads based on your device settings. You can stop this by resetting or deleting your advertising ID (see "Your choices" below).

Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (rated T or lower). Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it, for example which levels are started and completed, how long they take, and which hints and themes are used. Firebase Analytics collects this usage information together with an app instance ID, device and app information, and approximate location. It does not identify you by name.

The game also uses Firebase Remote Config to receive game settings (such as prices and how often ads appear) without an update.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or has an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends us a crash report: what went wrong in the game's code, your device model, Android version, app version and a Crashlytics installation ID. We use crash reports only to find and fix problems.

Learn more about Firebase and privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are handled by Google Play Billing. Your payment details are handled by Google and are never shared with us. The game only receives a record of the purchase so it can deliver what you bought and restore Remove Ads on a new install. See the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help delete it.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on your Android device, open Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some devices Settings › Google › Ads) and tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • Change your ad consent (EEA and UK): in the game, open Settings › Privacy choices.
  • Delete the data saved on your device: uninstall the game, or clear its data in your device's app settings.
  • For data held by Google, see the Google Privacy Policy and your Google account settings, or contact us for help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
